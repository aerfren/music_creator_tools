Music Creator Tools

A square cover, a YouTube thumbnail, a Shorts thumbnail, and a description you can paste. Two small portable tools, made for independent musicians.

Made by Penni (Fyuri) LeBlanc. Unzip a folder, keep the files together, and drag your picture or your song onto the .bat file.

Pictures

Thumbnail & Cover Maker

Drag and drop a picture. The tool makes a 1024×1024 square cover, a balanced YouTube thumbnail (16:9), and a YouTube Shorts thumbnail (9:16).

Set your artist name, keep every title line the same size, or switch on a Big Accent Line. A searchable picker lists every font installed on your Windows PC, and Oswald Bold is already in the folder.

  1. Unzip the download and keep the files in that folder together.
  2. Drop a PNG or JPG onto Make SunoMV Cover.bat.
  3. Type the title and artist, pick a font, and click Make thumbnails.

Words

YouTube Description Maker

Drag and drop an MP3 (the tool reads the ID3 title and the embedded lyrics) or a .txt lyrics file. You can also paste the lyrics yourself.

With your own free Gemini API key, it writes a clean description you can paste into YouTube. The finished text is saved to a file, copied to your clipboard, and opened in Notepad. Your lyrics stay word for word.

  1. Unzip the download and keep the files together.
  2. Open the bat file once and paste your free Gemini key. It stays on your computer.
  3. Drop an MP3 or lyrics file on Make YouTube Description.bat and click Write my description.
